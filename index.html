How it works

Three steps. No integration.

There is nothing to connect and nothing to install. If you can paste a link into a job posting, you can run this today.

1

We give you a link. It goes in your Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Facebook or Craigslist posting — wherever you already advertise. Applicants enter their name and number. That's the whole form.

2

By text and email, and into a spreadsheet if you want one. Read it in twenty seconds, listen to the recording if you're on the fence, and call the people worth your time.

Doing this properly

AI belongs in the first call, not the decision.

Neo conducts the interview and writes down what was said. It does not score candidates, rank them, or screen anybody out. There's no algorithm deciding who advances — a person reads the summary and decides, every time.

Every call opens by saying it's an automated assistant, and by saying it's recorded. Anyone who'd rather talk to a person can, and we'll show them how on the same page they applied on. Candidates are never asked about anything that isn't about the job.

Employers stay responsible for their own hiring decisions and for the questions they ask. We built it this way on purpose — a tool that decides for you is a tool that gets you sued.

Questions

Straight answers.

What if the applicant doesn't pick up?

Neo tries again — a few times, spread across the day, during daylight hours only. Most people answer, because they applied four minutes ago and they're expecting it. That's the whole advantage over calling them tomorrow afternoon.

Can I use my own questions?

Yes. Pick a role template to start — warehouse, caregiver, driver, call center, restaurant — then edit the questions or write your own. It's a text box, not a configuration project.

Does it work with my ATS?

It doesn't need to. Results arrive by text, email, and a spreadsheet, and there's a webhook if your operations person wants to wire it into something. If you need a deep Bullhorn or JobDiva integration, we're honestly not the right fit yet.

Does it sound like a robot?

What does the candidate experience feel like?

They apply, their phone rings a minute later, and a calm voice says it's an AI assistant calling about the job they just applied for and asks if now's a good time. Most people are pleasantly surprised that anybody called at all.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your account, without emailing anyone. There's no contract and no termination fee.
